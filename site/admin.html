Client Administration

Create clients, post documents to their data room, set their figures, and schedule calls. Everything here is private and access-logged.

Clients

Select a client to manage, or create a new one.

    New Client

    Creates the Cognito login and the portal profile. The one-time password is shown once — hand it over securely.

    Figures

    Shown on the client's Overview. Free text, so write them in the client's language.

    Post a Document

    PDF only. View-only files are watermarked and shown in-portal; downloadable files are watermarked PDFs.

    Schedule a Call

    Appears on the client's Calendar with a one-tap “Add to calendar”.
